Independent technology consulting

We assess, secure, and modernize the systems your business depends on

Eibit is an independent consulting company in Turin, Italy, founded in 2008. We work directly with the people who run your critical systems.

Services

  • Artificial intelligence (AI)

    We help you decide where AI creates value in your organization, and how to adopt and govern it.

  • Distributed ledger

    We evaluate ledger-based architectures for your use case, from feasibility studies to operational deployment.

  • Cybersecurity

    We assess your security risks, harden your systems, and establish the governance that keeps them dependable.

  • Cloud architecture

    We help you determine which workloads belong in the cloud and design an architecture that stays within your budget.

  • Systems integration

    We analyze how new platforms connect to your existing infrastructure, and tell you the true cost before you commit.

  • Legacy systems

    We maintain, refactor, and retire the legacy systems that carry your critical operations, without disruption.

About

We were founded in 2008 in Turin, Italy, and we are small by design. We take on a limited number of engagements at a time and decline work we are not equipped to do well. Each engagement is led by the consultant who started it, without hand-offs. We conduct much of the work under non-disclosure agreements, and we give client references by introduction, not by a public list.
